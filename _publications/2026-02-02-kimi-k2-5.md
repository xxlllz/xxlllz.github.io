Kimi K2.5 is an open-source, native multimodal agentic model built through continual pretraining on approximately 15 trillion mixed visual and text tokens atop Kimi-K2-Base, jointly optimizing text and vision so that the two modalities enhance each other.

- **arXiv**: [Kimi K2.5: Visual Agentic Intelligence](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.02276)
