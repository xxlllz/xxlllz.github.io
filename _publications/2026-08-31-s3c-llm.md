We propose S3C-LLM, a skill-guided and code-grounded agentic LLM for spectrum-to-structure elucidation. Rather than directly predicting a molecule, S3C-LLM retrieves modality-specific spectroscopy skills, executes analysis code to instantiate these skills on the input spectra, and integrates the results to infer the molecular structure.
